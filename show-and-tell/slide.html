My Ways

A food recommender that remembers how you feel.

By Kathryn Chiew
01What it is

A food recommender for Philly and NYC. It asks which city, then either shows me what just opened, or asks how I'm feeling, the occasion, who I'm with and what I want — and gives me one place to rate. Good picks go on my try list, and next time it asks how it went.

02The idea behind it

On its own, Claude can only guess where I should eat: it doesn't know my moods, what I've liked, or what opened this week. My server gives it that record, and the questions make me figure out what I actually want before it answers.

03One wrong thing, one fix

When I rated a recommendation badly, it banned that restaurant forever and made me answer all eight questions again. That's not what I want: now it asks what was wrong ("too loud"), keeps my other answers, and adjusts in that direction — and the place can come back another day when it fits.
